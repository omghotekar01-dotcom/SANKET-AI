# Privacy and Accessibility

- Camera and microphone are permission-gated by the browser.
- Normal recognition does not persist raw frames.
- Camera tracks are stopped when interpretation ends/unmounts.
- Facial landmarks are used only for sign-language/non-manual cues; no identity, emotion, ethnicity, disability or health inference is implemented.
- Feedback never retrains the live model automatically.
- Transcript updates are semantic text in an ARIA live region suitable for screen readers and refreshable Braille workflows.
- High contrast, large text and reduced motion are locally persisted.
- Haptic output is capability-detected and always paired with visible information.
- Emergency mode is a communication aid and does not claim dispatch.
